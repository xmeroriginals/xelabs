ntfy.sh for Scratch

Send instant push notifications from your Scratch projects

Quick Start

Follow these 3 steps to receive alerts on your phone or PC:

1

Pick a Topic

Choose a unique name like my_cool_program_99

2

Subscribe

Open the ntfy app or website and follow that topic.

3

Send Block

Use the extension blocks to send your message!

Block Reference

The easiest way to send a quick text. No setup required on the web side.

Advanced control: Add bold titles, emojis (tags), and set priority levels (1-5).

Priority Tip: Level 5 (Max) will bypass some "Do Not Disturb" settings and ring loudly!

Safety First:

ntfy.sh topics are public. If someone knows your topic name, they can read your notifications. Don't send passwords or private data!
